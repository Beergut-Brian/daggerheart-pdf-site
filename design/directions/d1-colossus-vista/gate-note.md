# d1-colossus-vista — gate note

**What:** the Brine Colossus is the whole painted scene; the live salt pours from its raised hand onto HIGH SODIUM GAMING and down onto the RESOURCES plaque.
**Axis value:** monster as huge backdrop.
**Signature test:** swap in a coffee shop's name: a salt giant pouring salt on "Bean There Café" makes no sense. Fails = pass.
**Exclude check:** a painted scene and a character carry the page; delete the scrim and plaque and the point still stands. No frames, rules, rings or glyph circles.
**Strongest part:** desktop first screen. The painted stream under the hand runs straight into the live pour, so the painting and the page read as one scene. The gold heart and the sunset do the backlight.
**Real risk:** the salt piles. The pour rate is tuned so salt reaches the plaque inside 4.5 s, but on a phone the plaque pile grows tall enough to cross the tagline (salt-2.png). Fix before build: more gap above the plaque, or a lower rate. The engine's chunky 2x2 grains also add to the blocky look.
**Weak image (honest):** next to the real covers (steer-1/05, 06) the golem still reads blocky, close to "Minecraft". I cropped in (zoom 1.06 on phone, 1.1 on desktop), pushed the gold backlight and brine glow with screen-blend light, and added a vignette and drifting mist. That gets it to "big painted vista". It does not reach the covers' energy: they have a cast in motion, dramatic perspective and a bright sky behind a giant title, and ours is one calm, symmetrical robot standing still. The phone crop (d1-hero-tall) is the weaker painting: flatter, with the head small against a big arm. A regenerated, more organic and dynamic Colossus would lift this board more than any CSS.
**Fonts:** Cinzel Decorative 900 (wordmark, HSG), DM Serif Display (headings, tagline), Alegreya Sans (UI). All OFL, from Google Fonts.
**Salt:** cell 3 phone / 4 desktop; maxGrains 6000 / 14000; rate 150 / 420 per s for 2.8 s; prefill 450 / 1200; glyphBridge -0.07 (opens the letter gaps so salt falls through the name onto the plaque). Emitter measured from the paintings: wide (0.456, 0.303) under the raised hand, tall (0.39, 0.176) under the cupped fingers. hero.js keeps the anchor on that point through object-fit cover, object-position and the zoom, and centres the type column under the pour. Shared engine unchanged.
**Resources:** real covers (Core large, Hope & Fear beside it), a static salt dusting on both cover tops (same engine, no emitter), the cut-out Colossus perched on Hope & Fear (once, small), the "Alt covers (ours)" strip, and the 6-item "Sample shelf: future titles" (titles 3 to 6 use crops of D art with the title set in HTML).
**Viewer:** indigo chrome; the parchment page is the brightest thing on screen; gold ring on page 19. No monster (kept quiet).
**Detect:** fixed wide tracking on the book meta. Kept, with reasons: dark-glow / radial-halo (dark indigo legibility shadow and scrim behind type over art, not a coloured glow); repeating-stripes (the grey text lines of the placeholder book page and thumbnails); cramped-padding (tab underline and phone-frame edges are flush on purpose); flat-type-hierarchy (small titles in the sample-shelf scale test).
