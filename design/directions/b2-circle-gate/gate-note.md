# b2-circle-gate — Salt Circle, threshold

- **What it is:** the HIGH SODIUM GAMING wordmark is free and huge, fitted edge to edge (one line on desktop, three width-filling lines on a phone). Below it, a small, tight salt ward is drawn around `{ RESOURCES }`. The button crosses the ring like a lintel through two gates. The rest of the hero is a quiet field of strewn salt that gathers toward the ward.
- **Variation axis:** ring scale and role = **gate around the button** (small, about 22rem). The wordmark carries the scale and the ring carries the meaning.
- **Signature test: pass, but closer than b1.** The strewn salt and the ward around the button still read as "salt table". With a coffee shop's name in place, the field could pass for spilled sugar, so the ring and glyphs do all the work. That is why b1 is the bolder pick.
- **Strongest part:** the wordmark. R19-style, it spans the width in gilt Cinzel Decorative with SODIUM in salt-white Cinzel caps, and the only call to action is literally inside the ward.
- **Real risk:** on desktop the middle of the screen is quiet (the field is sparse by design) and the ring is small, so it can read as too calm for Kaden's "boring before wrong". The wordmark fit uses a measure-once script (the builder can do the same, or use fixed clamps per breakpoint).
- **Resources / viewer:** the same as b1 (same covers, grain outline, active-thumbnail ring, loading ring) but without the 6-book scale section, as the brief asks.
- **Fonts (OFL):** Cinzel Decorative 900, Cinzel 500/700, Alegreya Sans.
- **Motion:** salt settles over 0.6 s, the ward pours round the button from 0.35 s to 1.55 s, glyphs wake by about 1.8 s, then a 14 s breath. Reduced motion shows the static finished state.
- **Detect:** fixed the same leaks as b1. **Kept: 3× gradient-text on index.html** (the deliberate R21 three-band gilt wordmark).
