# site-strategy

A strategy says who the site is for, what it must get them to do, and what pages and content that takes. Design and build follow it. Kaden approves it before design starts.

## Steps

1. **Discovery** from the brief and `asset-intake` findings: the business, services or programs, area, differentiators, proof (reviews, licenses, years, warranties), current site and its problems, goals and the one metric that matters (calls, quotes, donations).
2. **Audience**: two or three visitor types, each with the question they arrive with and the action they should take. Example for roofing: homeowner with storm damage (urgent, wants a fast inspection), homeowner planning a replacement (comparing, wants trust and price clarity), property manager (wants reliability and paperwork).
3. **Competitor review**: look at three to five local competitors' sites. Note what they claim, how they convert, what they do well, and gaps. Facts from their public pages only; cite URLs. No copying of copy or design.
4. **Positioning**: one sentence the whole site supports, backed by the proof the brief actually has. Mark anything unverified `[CONFIRM]`.
5. **Conversion plan**: primary action and how it appears on every page; secondary actions; trust elements placed next to each CTA; response-time promise if the brief allows one; tracking goals.
6. **Site map**: every page with URL slug, purpose, audience served, primary CTA, and priority (launch vs later). Follow the site profile's default pages and add only what the business needs. Service-area pages only where the brief lists areas.
7. **Page briefs**: for each page, the sections in order, each with its job in one line and the content it needs (facts, photos, proof). This is what `design-direction` and `content` work from.
8. **Content plan**: what exists, what the client must supply, what the agent drafts for approval. Photo needs per page (see `imagery`).
9. **SEO targets**: the service and location terms each page is for, one primary per page, from the brief's services and areas. No keyword research tools unless the brief provides access; say so.
10. **Phases**: launch set vs later additions, with the done tests from the profile.

## Output

`strategy.md` using `references/strategy-template.md`. Under 4 pages. Then a Blocked entry: "Strategy ready for approval." Design does not start until Kaden approves or edits it.

## Rules

Claims only from the brief or cited competitor pages. No invented statistics, awards, or reviews. Where the brief is silent on a business matter (pricing display, financing, emergency service), ask; do not decide.
