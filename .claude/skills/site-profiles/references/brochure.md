# Profile: brochure

Main visitor action: contact, or trust the business enough to visit or call.

## Required facts
Business description, offerings, location(s) and hours, contact methods, brand assets, proof (reviews, clients, press).

## Default pages
Home, about, services or products overview, contact, plus shared defaults. Gallery or FAQ if the brief asks.

## Extra skills
`forms-and-leads` for the contact form only. `seo-setup` standard section, plus local section if the business has a physical location.

## Phase order
0 Foundation → 1 Design and pages → 2 Contact and SEO → 3 QA and launch.

## Done tests
Contact form delivers; every page passes the page checklist; Lighthouse performance at or above 90 on mobile.
