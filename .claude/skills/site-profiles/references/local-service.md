# Profile: local-service

Main visitor action: request a quote, book, or call.

## Required facts from the brief
- Services offered, grouped as the business groups them
- Service area (cities, counties, or radius) and whether work is on-site or in-shop
- Primary conversion: call, quote form, online booking (and the booking tool if any), or chat
- Hours, phone, address, licenses or certifications, insurance, years in business
- Proof: reviews source, photos, before/after, guarantees
- Pricing approach: published prices, starting-at, or quote only

## Default pages
Home, one page per service (or per service group), service-area page(s), about, reviews or gallery, contact/quote, FAQ, plus shared defaults. Seasonal or promo landing pages if the brief lists campaigns.

## Extra skills
- `forms-and-leads`: quote form, booking, click-to-call, lead routing and notifications, spam protection
- `seo-setup` local section: LocalBusiness schema, NAP consistency, service-area pages, Google Business Profile alignment
- `analytics-tracking`: call and form conversions as goals
- `integrations` if a CRM, scheduler, or review platform is connected

## Phase order
0 Foundation → 1 Design and pages → 2 Lead capture and routing → 3 Local SEO → 4 QA and launch.

## Done tests
- Phone number and address match everywhere, including schema (NAP)
- A quote form submission reaches the owner's inbox or CRM within a minute and the visitor sees confirmation
- Every service page has one clear CTA above the fold on mobile
- Service-area pages exist for each listed area without duplicate copy
- Lighthouse mobile performance at or above 85

## Patterns
Phone number in the header on mobile; trust signals near every CTA; one form, reused with hidden "service" field rather than one form per page.
