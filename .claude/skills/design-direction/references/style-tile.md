# Style tile contents (one screen each)

1. Name of the direction and one sentence on the feeling.
2. Palette: background, surface, text, primary, accent, with roles and contrast ratios.
3. Type: heading and body faces, the scale, a sample headline and paragraph.
4. Shape and depth: radius, borders, shadows, on a button, a card, and an input.
5. Hero sample: headline, subhead, CTA, image treatment.
6. Photography and iconography treatment: tone, crop, overlay, icon style.
7. Motion: hover and transition notes in one line.
8. Where each choice came from in the reference inventory.
